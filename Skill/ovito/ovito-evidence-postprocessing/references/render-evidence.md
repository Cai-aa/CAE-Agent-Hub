# OVITO Render Evidence

- Input trajectory exists, is readable, and reports expected frames and atoms.
- Pipeline and modifier settings are saved in a script or state file.
- Reference frame and cutoff are recorded for local strain or displacement.
- Color legend, units, scalar range, and clipping/selection rules are visible or documented.
- Camera and viewport do not hide the mechanism being discussed.
- Animation frame interval and physical timestep mapping are stated.
- Numeric exports support quantitative claims; screenshots alone support only visual observations.
- Upstream LAMMPS logs remain the solve evidence.

If the image was generated by Matplotlib or a custom parser, label it as an
auxiliary visualization rather than an OVITO result.
